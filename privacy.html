Intaglio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Intaglio is a tool for sharing your own contact details and links in person. It is built so that nothing you put into it ever leaves your phone unless you choose to show it to someone.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party code. The app makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile and cards — the name, role, contact details and links you enter. Stored on your device, and shared only with the app's own Lock Screen widget so it can show your default card.
  • People you scan — the contents of any code you scan, plus the event name and any note you add. Stored on your device only.

You can delete any of this at any time from within the app, and deleting the app removes all of it.

What other people receive

Only what you show them. A QR code contains exactly the card you chose: a link, or a contact card with the details you entered. What happens after someone scans it — opening a website, saving a contact — happens on their device, under their control.

Permissions

  • Camera — used only while the scanner is open, to read QR codes. No images are saved.
  • Contacts — used only when you choose to save someone you scanned, through the system's own contact sheet.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
